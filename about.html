Research

In BRIDGE-bench, I built an eval of LLM reasoning on real bridge exploits, then audited the benchmark itself. Thirteen of 24 prompts leaked bug descriptions, and changing the judge moved the score by 14.5 points. I treated that as the result: added prompt sanitization, matched controls, dataset-integrity checks, and judge-validity tests rather than keeping a contaminated headline metric.

In active-materials-discovery, active learning reached ~5.15× discovery acceleration on matbench_perovskites, but MC-dropout uncertainty was miscalibrated (Spearman ρ = -0.47 against absolute error). The gain came from the model mean, not the uncertainty bonus. That negative result led directly into upstream MatGL work.
