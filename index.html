current work
  • AI evaluations — separate capability from dataset metadata.

    BRIDGE-bench's historical score is contaminated evidence after the 13/24 leakage audit. Current work is zero-cost measurement design, sanitizer invariants, and matched controls; there is no claimed sanitized rerun.
